LAND SURVEYING

Know your land
with clarity.

Land surveying provides measured information about a property, its boundaries and its existing physical conditions. At SR Associates, field measurements are organized into clear survey information that can support planning, development, construction and documentation.

Depending on the project requirement, survey observations can be used for boundary identification, plot measurement, demarcation, area calculation and detailed site measurement.

01 / BOUNDARY

Boundary Survey

Measurement and documentation of property boundaries and relevant site points as required for the project.

02 / MEASUREMENT

Plot Measurement

Detailed measurement of land dimensions, corners and important physical features of the site.

03 / AREA

Area Calculation

Calculation of measured site areas from collected survey coordinates and dimensions.

04 / DEMARCATION

Demarcation

Identification and marking of required points or boundaries on the ground according to project requirements.

05 / SITE DATA

Existing Site Survey

Collection of existing ground and site information for planning, design and construction activities.

06 / DATA

Survey Documentation

Organized survey information and drawings prepared from measured field data for practical project use.

ON-SITE MEASUREMENT

Field data that
supports the site.

A land survey begins with accurate field observations. The required points and features are measured on site and then organized into usable survey information.

  • Property and plot dimensions
  • Boundary and corner points
  • Existing site features
  • Measured coordinates and levels where required
  • Area and dimensional information
  • Survey drawings and digital outputs
