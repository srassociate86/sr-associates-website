01 / GNSS & RTK

Know exactly where you are.

GNSS surveying allows survey points to be positioned using satellite observations. RTK workflows can provide highly precise real-time positioning when suitable correction data and field conditions are available.

  • Coordinate & control point collection
  • Topographical point survey
  • Construction layout
  • RTK field data collection

02 / TOTAL STATION

Precision from line of sight.

Total stations combine angular and distance measurements to determine precise positions in the field. They remain particularly useful where line-of-sight measurements are required or satellite visibility is limited.

  • Detailed topographical surveys
  • Construction setting out
  • Building and structure surveys
  • Precise distance and angle measurement

03 / DRONE PHOTOGRAMMETRY

See the entire site from above.

Drone imagery can cover large areas efficiently. Photogrammetric processing converts overlapping aerial photographs into measurable digital mapping products.

  • Orthomosaic mapping
  • Digital terrain models
  • Elevation information
  • 3D reconstruction

04 / DIGITAL MAPPING

From survey points to 3D data.

Field observations and aerial data can be transformed into CAD drawings, contours, surfaces, profiles, sections and 3D terrain information for project planning and analysis.
