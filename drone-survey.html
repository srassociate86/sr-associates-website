PHOTOGRAMMETRY WORKFLOW

Images become
spatial information.

Photogrammetry uses overlapping photographs and processing workflows to reconstruct the surveyed area. The final result depends on flight planning, image quality, overlap, ground control where required, processing settings and site conditions.

Flight Planning

The aerial survey is planned according to the site area, terrain, required coverage and intended project outputs.

Image Acquisition

Overlapping photographs are collected across the survey area to provide suitable coverage for photogrammetric processing.

Ground Control

Ground control points can be incorporated when required by the project to help reference and check the resulting data.

Processing

Images are processed to generate mapping, surface, orthomosaic and three-dimensional outputs as required.

01 / MAP Orthomosaic

Georeferenced aerial image output for visual mapping and project documentation.

02 / SURFACE Terrain / Surface

Digital elevation or surface information generated from the processed survey data.

03 / MODEL 3D Model

Three-dimensional reconstruction for visualization and suitable project applications.

04 / DATA Digital Outputs

Processed imagery and spatial data for further mapping or CAD-based workflows where required.
