GNSS POSITIONING

Precise coordinates
for real projects.

GNSS RTK surveying uses satellite positioning with real-time corrections to collect accurate coordinates in the field. It is useful where reliable horizontal and vertical position information is required across a site.

At SR Associates, GNSS RTK survey data can be used for control points, topographical mapping, georeferencing, construction-related work and large-area surveys according to project requirements and site conditions.

01 / RTK

RTK Positioning

Real-time corrected GNSS positioning for collecting accurate field coordinates where suitable correction services are available.

02 / CONTROL

Control Points

Establishment or observation of survey control points for mapping, construction and project reference.

03 / COORDINATES

Coordinate Collection

Collection of point coordinates for survey mapping, measurement and further CAD processing.

04 / GEOREFERENCING

Georeferencing

Positioning survey or mapping information within a defined coordinate reference system as required.

05 / LARGE AREA

Large-Area Survey

Efficient coordinate collection across larger sites and areas where GNSS positioning is suitable.

GNSS RTK WORKFLOW

Positioning depends on
good field conditions.

RTK performance depends on satellite visibility, correction availability, observation conditions and the survey requirement. Survey observations are therefore collected and checked according to the conditions at the site.

Satellite Visibility

Open sky conditions generally provide better satellite visibility and can support stable GNSS observations.

RTK Corrections

Real-time correction information is used where available and suitable for the required positioning workflow.

Coordinate System

Survey points can be organized using the coordinate system and reference framework required for the project.

Field Verification

Survey observations can be reviewed and checked before the final coordinate data is prepared for project use.

01 / POSITION Horizontal

Collection of plan-position coordinates according to the project requirement and field conditions.

02 / ELEVATION Vertical

GNSS-derived elevation information where the survey workflow and project reference system support it.

03 / QUALITY Field Checks

Observations can be checked using available positioning quality information before delivery.
