TOTAL STATION SURVEYING

Measure the site
with precision.

A total station combines electronic angle and distance measurement to collect detailed survey observations. It is widely used where controlled point measurement, detailed site information and setting-out work are required.

At SR Associates, total station surveys can support existing site surveys, construction layout, alignment, setting out, detailed measurement and other engineering requirements.

01 / SITE

Detailed Site Survey

Collection of measured points and existing features for detailed representation of a project site.

02 / SETTING OUT

Construction Layout

Transfer of required design or project points to the ground for construction-related activities.

03 / ALIGNMENT

Alignment

Establishment and checking of lines, points and alignments according to project requirements.

04 / MEASUREMENT

Precise Measurement

Electronic measurement of angles and distances for required survey points and site features.

05 / LEVELS

Point & Level Data

Collection of point information that can support site drawings, levels and further survey processing.

06 / ENGINEERING

Engineering Survey

Survey support for suitable engineering, construction and infrastructure applications.

TOTAL STATION CAPABILITIES

One instrument.
Many site applications.

Total station surveying can be adapted to different site requirements, from detailed existing-condition measurement to construction setting out and alignment work.

Angle & Distance

Electronic horizontal and vertical angle observations combined with distance measurement provide point-based survey information.

Point Positioning

Measured observations can be used to establish or record required points relative to the project survey reference.

Construction Setting Out

Design points and construction references can be transferred to the ground according to the available project data.

Site Verification

Survey observations can support checking of positions, alignments and measured site conditions during project work.

01 / SURVEY Existing Conditions

Capture measured points and relevant physical site features.

02 / SET OUT Design to Ground

Establish required project points and alignments on site.

03 / CHECK Site Verification

Measure and check selected points against the project requirements.
