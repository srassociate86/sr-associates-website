WHAT WE DO

Accurate data for better decisions.

At SR Associates, we combine field surveying expertise with modern measurement and mapping technology to provide reliable spatial data. From a small land parcel to large-area topographical mapping, our work is focused on accuracy, clear documentation and practical project deliverables.

01

Land Survey

Accurate measurement and documentation of land parcels, boundaries and existing site conditions for property, planning, development and construction requirements.

Boundary Survey Plot Measurement Area Calculation Demarcation Site Measurement

02

Topographical Survey

Detailed survey of natural and man-made features, elevations and terrain conditions to create an accurate representation of the existing ground.

Contours Spot Levels Terrain Mapping Existing Features Site Plans Items of Work

03

DGPS/ GNSS / RTK Survey

High-accuracy positioning and coordinate collection using GNSS RTK technology for control points, mapping, georeferencing and large-area survey applications.

RTK Positioning Control Points Coordinates Georeferencing Large Area Survey

04

Total Station Survey

Precise angular and distance measurements for detailed site surveys, construction work, alignment, setting out and engineering applications.

Site Survey Construction Layout Setting Out Alignment Detailed Measurement

05

Drone Survey & Photogrammetry

Aerial data collection for efficient mapping of large areas, generating orthomosaics, terrain models and three-dimensional project information.

Aerial Mapping Orthomosaic Photogrammetry DTM 3D Models Points Cloud

06

CAD & 3D Mapping

Conversion of field survey data into clear digital drawings, plans, sections and three-dimensional models suitable for design, planning and documentation.

CAD Drawings Contour Plans Sections Surface Models 3D Mapping

07

Earthwork Quantity

Survey-based calculation of excavation, filling and earthwork quantities using measured ground surfaces and digital terrain data.

Cut & Fill Excavation Volume Calculation Stockpile Volume Surface Comparison Cross Sections Longitudinal Section
