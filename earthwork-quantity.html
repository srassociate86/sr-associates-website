WHAT WE DO

Accurate quantities start with accurate ground data.

At SR Associates, surveyed ground levels and digital terrain information can be used to assess excavation, filling and other earthwork quantities. Existing ground surfaces can be compared with required levels or project surfaces to understand the amount and location of earthwork.

Earthwork quantities depend on the survey coverage, point density, surface definition and the reference or proposed levels used for the calculation. The final method and deliverables can be selected according to the project requirements.
