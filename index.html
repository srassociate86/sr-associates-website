WHAT WE DO

Our Survey Services

Professional field surveying and digital mapping solutions for land, infrastructure and construction.

Land Survey

Boundary, property and plot surveys for site planning, construction and land documentation. Accurate field measurements establish positions, dimensions and site control.

Topographical Survey

Detailed surveys capturing contours, spot elevations and existing site features for terrain modelling, drainage planning, construction design and earthwork calculations.

DGPS / GNSS / RTK Survey

High-accuracy DGPS, GNSS and RTK positioning for control points, boundary work, mapping and site measurements, providing precise coordinates for survey and engineering workflows.

Total Station Survey

Precise total station measurements for construction layout, control surveys, structural positioning and site detail where reliable angles, distances and elevations are required.

Drone Survey & Photogrammetry

Aerial surveying for orthomosaics, digital terrain models, surface models and 3D reconstruction, helping cover large or difficult sites efficiently.

CAD & 3D Mapping

Survey data converted into clear CAD drawings, plans, sections and 3D mapping outputs for design, quantity calculations, construction planning and documentation.

Earthwork Quantity

Cut-and-fill calculations, excavation quantities and terrain volume analysis from survey and surface data for estimating earthwork requirements and comparing ground levels.
